Geonosis Front

22 BBY. Seven missions through the First Battle of Geonosis, from the rescue in the Petranaki arena and the strike on the launch hives to the charge of the walkers, the core ships and the fall of the droid foundries. You command the Grand Army of the Republic.

Seize the five control points across the spire fields, keep requisition flowing, and destroy the enemy command centre: the Republic forward base, or the Separatist core ship.

      The First Battle of Geonosis

      The Republic stands on the edge of war. Count Dooku and his Separatist movement have gathered their droid armies on the desert world of Geonosis, deep in the Outer Rim.

      Captured while investigating the Separatist foundries, Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi has been condemned to die in the Petranaki arena, alongside Anakin Skywalker and Senator Padmé Amidala.

      Master Mace Windu leads a strike team of Jedi to free them, while Master Yoda races across the galaxy with a newly discovered army of clone troopers....
